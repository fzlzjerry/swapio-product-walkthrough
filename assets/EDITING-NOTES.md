# 30-second editing sample breakdown

The release asset `swapio-editing-sample-30s.mp4` is an exact 30-second cut of
the longer product walkthrough. It is intended as a pause-frameable teaching
sample rather than a generic showreel.

## Timeline

- **00:00–00:03 — Hook:** the promise is compressed into a large two-line claim,
  with the live-test/no-funds boundary visible before the viewer has to infer it.
- **00:03–00:07 — Motion transition:** the opening typography eases out while
  the real product surface scales and fades in under the brand lockup.
- **00:07–00:15 — Evidence scene:** a live quote is framed beside concise context;
  the lower caption reinforces the important state without covering the UI.
- **00:15–00:20 — Graphic transition:** the product capture yields to a clean
  comparison board so four close values can be read rather than rushed.
- **00:20–00:27 — Data emphasis:** staggered route bars and color-coded deltas
  reveal the small spread while the narration explains why visibility matters.
- **00:27–00:30 — Scene handoff:** the comparison resolves into the recurring-order
  section, demonstrating a third visual grammar instead of repeating one layout.

## Techniques demonstrated

1. **Caption placement:** persistent lower-third captions sit inside a protected
   safe area, separate from primary titles and real product controls.
2. **Transitions:** scale/fade product reveals, board-to-board crossfades, and
   directional/staggered data motion are used for different information changes.
3. **Color treatment:** a consistent near-black grade, restrained purple/blue
   bloom, high-contrast text, and green/yellow evidence accents unify mixed captures.
4. **Audio synchronization:** narration clauses align with the corresponding
   evidence scene and visual emphasis; scene cuts occur in the natural pauses.
5. **First-three-second hook:** value proposition and evidence boundary arrive
   immediately, before branding or background explanation.
